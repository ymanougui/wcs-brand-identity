# WeChinaSourcing brand identity

## Source of truth
- GitHub: https://github.com/ymanougui/wcs-brand-identity (main).
- Local checkout when available: `D:\Coding projects\wcs-brand-identity`.
- Use a verified current backup if the master repository cannot be accessed.

## Workflow
1. Read the latest README.md and AGENTS.md from the master repository.
2. Consult WeChinaSourcing-Brand-Guidelines.pdf or its HTML equivalent and Quick-Start-Sheet.pdf or its HTML equivalent.
3. Locate official logos, tokens and vectors in brand-assets/ and approved photography in Images/. Do not use inspiration images as approved assets without verification.
4. Use the exact approved assets and typography, respecting color roles, logo clear space and layout rules. Use brand-assets/02-color/tokens.json and tokens.css; README.md lists conflicting colors, so verify against the official guidelines and disclose unresolved conflicts.
5. Create the requested deliverable, checking brand consistency, legibility and logo integrity. Do not invent brand assets or change standards without permission.
6. If a required asset is inaccessible, disclose that limitation; do not silently substitute branding.
7. Apply the brand to WCS deliverables only, not unrelated companies or client brands unless requested.
