# WeChinaSourcing brand identity instructions

This repository is the official reference for WeChinaSourcing (WCS) branding. Consult it before producing WCS documents, quotations, invoices, catalogs, presentations, websites, social media, marketing materials or visual designs.

## Workflow
- Read README.md and review WeChinaSourcing-Brand-Guidelines.pdf (or its HTML equivalent). Use Quick-Start-Sheet.pdf or its HTML equivalent for rapid reference.
- Locate approved logos, vectors and tokens in brand-assets/. Use approved photography in Images/ where relevant. Inspiration images are references, not automatically approved production assets.
- Use official logo variants, typography, spacing, colors and templates as specified in the guidelines. Do not redraw, distort or substitute brand assets without explicit approval.
- Verified core palette from README.md: Deep Navy #0A192F; Teal / Cyan #00D2B4; Porcelain White #FEFEFE; Slate Gray #64748B. Consult the full guidelines for usage and other specifications.
- Use the latest accessible repository version; if official assets or rules cannot be retrieved, disclose the limitation before making branding assumptions.
- Preserve existing brand assets. Do not change brand standards unless explicitly requested.
- Check final deliverables for brand consistency, logo integrity, legibility and appropriate layout.
